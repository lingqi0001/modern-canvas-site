A fresher look for Canvas

Soft corners, colours you choose yourself, and a set of study tools that fit into the pages you already open every day.

See the five screens What it does

What changes on your Canvas

Every part is a switch you can turn off. The pages underneath stay exactly where Canvas put them.

A softer, rounder interface

Dashboard, calendar, grades, syllabus, groups, announcements and conversations all get redrawn with rounded corners and one consistent colour set.

Search everything at once

One box finds courses, work, announcements, pages, links and messages. It reads from an index built on your own machine, so typing sends no requests.

An inbox that reads like mail

Threads open in a two column view, links and files inside them are tracked by type, and a long message gets its dates pulled out into one line.

One to do panel

Assignments, announcements, feedback and finished work sit in the same list, grouped by when they are due, with your progress across the week on top.

Themes, including your own

Browse thousands of community themes with their colours and card images shown up front, edit any of the twelve colour slots, or import a theme file.

A sidebar you can arrange

Drag the navigation rows into the order you want, move them between the two areas, and hide the ones you never open.

How it behaves

The rules the whole extension is built on.

Text stays readable

Every colour you pick is checked against the surfaces it lands on. When contrast drops too low, the text on that surface flips to black or white on its own.

It plays nice

If BetterCampus is installed too, you choose which one paints the page. Layout fixes, panels and colours stay independent of each other either way.

No servers of ours

The optional AI summaries talk straight to the provider whose key you enter, and they ask you before sending anything large. We have no backend to send it to.

Get Modern Canvas

Works in Chrome and Edge on any Canvas site at instructure.com. No account, no sign in, nothing to configure before it starts helping.
